**Candid** is a minimalist, privacy-first journaling app that helps users understand what they write about and verify the accuracy of their claims. All entries and data are stored locally on the device, while lightweight AI endpoints provide topic classification and optional fact-checking.

---

## Features

### Simple Local Journaling  
Write and save entries directly on your device.

### Automatic Topic Classification  
After submitting an entry, Candid assigns categories using an external `/classify` endpoint. Categories help users see patterns in their writing.

### Fact Check Your Claims  
Highlight any portion of an entry and tap **Fact Check**. Candid sends the selected text to the `/factcheck` endpoint, gathers supporting sources, and displays them as evidence linked to the entry.

### Category Blocks View  
A treemap-style mosaic visually displays your most frequent writing topics using dynamically sized blocks.

### Search  
Find entries instantly with full-text, on-device search.

### Daily Quote  
A randomly selected quote appears every time the app is opened.
